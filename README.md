# Tesla Financial Analysis, Valuation & Risk Assessment

### Project Overview

This independent financial analysis project evaluates Tesla's historical financial performance, future growth potential, intrinsic valuation, and financial risk using Excel-based financial modeling.

The objective is to apply financial analysis and risk management techniques to understand Tesla's financial position, identify potential vulnerabilities, and evaluate its long-term performance under different assumptions.

### Completed Analysis

**Phase 1: Historical Financial Performance**
- Analyzed Tesla's financial statements and operating performance.
- Evaluated revenue growth, profitability, and historical financial trends.

**Phase 2: Financial Statement Analysis**
- Examined Tesla's income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement.
- Evaluated financial health, capital structure, and cash generation.

**Phase 3: Financial Forecasting & Valuation**
- Developed financial projections through 2030.
- Built a discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation model.
- Estimated the weighted average cost of capital (WACC).
- Conducted sensitivity analysis and bull, base, and bear scenario testing.

**Phase 4: Financial Risk Assessment**
- Evaluated liquidity, solvency, and financial leverage.
- Conducted revenue, operating cost, and cash flow stress testing.
- Built a Monte Carlo simulation with 1,000 potential free cash flow outcomes.
- Developed a financial risk scoring matrix.

### Tools & Methods

Microsoft Excel, financial statement analysis, forecasting, DCF valuation, CAPM, WACC, scenario analysis, stress testing, Monte Carlo simulation, and financial risk scoring.
